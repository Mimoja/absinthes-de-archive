## Beschreibung

Ein Absinth Löffel in der Form einer Wermutpflanze.

Er hat neun Löcher und erscheint als Wermutblatt. In die Oberfläche des Kopfteils sind Blattstränge graviert.

Ein sehr schönes Stück.

Dieser Löffel wird in Frankreich hergestellt.

Kopf

Gehöhlt, eher ungewöhnlich für einen Absinth Löffel.

### ★★★★☆ Dan M · 2013-05-16

Positives:
+ Very beautiful and elegant design. A lot of attention is given to details.
+ Well-balanced, sits perfectly on top of the glass.
+ The gold finish looks and feels fantastic.
+ Does what it's supposed to do.

Negatives:
- Holes are a bit small, which may cause it to catch water every now and then, which then gets released as a stream. Not particularly pleasant if you want to admire the louche.
- Catches prints very easily, even when leaving to dry after washing. Needs a bit of maintenance.

Overall, a good product for the price, but not excellent. Enough to give it four stars, though, and to warmly recommend it to anyone who wants to add a bit of elegance to their absinthe enjoyment.
