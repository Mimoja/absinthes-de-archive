---
Reply from Andrew at Absinthes.com:

Hello Echo, I'm sorry to hear about your glass. In our FAQ we advise that absinthe should never be set on fire - http://www.absinthes.com/faq.php?faq=14§ion=25#43 - it's not at all the traditional way of preparing an absinthe, and it can damage the flavour of the absinthe as well. Not many glasses, hand or machine made, will necessarily survive the extreme and rapid temperature change of fire to ice, either.

We would always recommend this way to best appreciate an absinthe drink: pour one measure of absinthe into a glass and then drip three to five parts chilled water into it, over a spoon with a sugar cube if desired. Enjoy!
